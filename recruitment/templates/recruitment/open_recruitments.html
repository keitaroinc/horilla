Why Keitaro?

We're expanding quickly and continuously seeking outstanding individuals like you. Whether you're a junior or a senior, we at Keitaro believe that every person has a unique talent and potential. If you are the right cultural fit we can achieve great things together.

We consistently put the needs of the employees first, and we also believe that having a comfortable workplace benefits everyone.

Workspace & Culture

Thanks to our vibrant working environment, we remain energised, driven and united in our passion for big ideas. Our focus on performance encourages each team member to push themselves and reach new milestones every day.

We also encourage and motivate our team to support the wider tech community, through transparency and through our open-source contributions as well.

As a team and as individuals, we strive to learn and grow on a personal and professional level. Side-by-side, we deliver results, and of course, have lots of fun too!
